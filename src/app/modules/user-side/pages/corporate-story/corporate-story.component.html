Our Corporate Story

In January 2022, the seeds of our company were sown when three directors, hailing from the bustling city of Dar es Salaam, brought together a multidisciplinary team with backgrounds in business, technology, and finance. United by common technological challenges and a shared vision for Tanzania's future, they embarked on a journey to establish an innovative IT company known as "CHARIOTECH” in May 2022.

Driven by passion, enthusiasm, and a belief in the transformative power of emerging computing technologies, the co-founders set out to lay the foundation for CHARIOTECH's success.
