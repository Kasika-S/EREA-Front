Our mandate

Regional cooperation, practical outcomes

Making regional energy regulation work across borders.

EREA supports cooperation among East African energy regulators, helping member states build compatible frameworks for reliable, affordable, and sustainable energy.

Through policy dialogue, knowledge sharing, regulatory capacity building, and shared evidence, we help turn regional ambitions into coordinated action for people, businesses, and the energy sector.

What we do

A common foundation for an interconnected energy market.

EREA focuses on the regulatory conditions that make regional energy collaboration more effective and more investable.

01

Harmonise regulation

Support compatible standards, approaches, and regulatory practice across member states.

02

Strengthen institutions

Create spaces for peer learning, technical exchange, and stronger regulatory capability.

03

Turn data into insight

Make regional energy information easier to access, compare, and use in decision-making.

Part of a bigger vision

Advancing the East African Community Energy Union.

EREA contributes to a regional energy system where electricity, petroleum, renewable energy, and regulatory knowledge can move more confidently across borders.
